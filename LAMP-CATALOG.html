At its own low estimate of $650 it lands near $1,010 and scores 0.64, which is a bad buy. The estimate is above what these actually fetch.

Max bid $228. That is the hammer at which it still meets the excellent-value threshold. Above $329 it stops being worth publishing at all. The lot is already at $300 — there is roughly $29 of headroom left, and it may simply run away. Register 48 hours ahead if you want the option.

Condition passes. Shade perfect. Base shows light wear to patina, which is preferred over a polished one. Working, but the original cord should be rewired — expected, and priced in above. Base stamped Pairpoint C3064, which is the right mark in the right place.

Authenticity: good. “Blown-out floral designs on latticework” describes a genuine moulded puffy, not a painted imitation of one. The stamped model number on the base is consistent with factory work. Not yet confirmed: whether shade and base are original to each other — worth asking, since a boudoir lamp of this value is a common candidate for marriage.

Why the estimate misleads. Three of the four comparable sales landed under $800 against estimates as high as $2,500. Houses estimate puffies optimistically. Bid the comps, not the estimate. View lot →
