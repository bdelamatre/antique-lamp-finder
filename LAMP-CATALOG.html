Toomey & Co. and Rago both newly blocked

403 · both Blocks §5 scoring Workaround found

Tested four ways today: a Rago lot page from 2021, the Rago lot page that supplied a run-1 comp, the equivalent Toomey lot page, and the Toomey site root. All four returned 403 Forbidden. This is not one stale URL — it is the whole of both domains, and both fetched normally on 27 July.

These were the comp sources. Run 1’s headline improvement was finding them; §5 forbids scoring without three dated realized sales, and eBay sold is login-walled, Invaluable 403s, Worthpoint is paywalled. Losing Toomey and Rago on the same day removes the routine’s ability to value anything it has not already valued.

The workaround: query the search index against those domains rather than fetching them. Realized prices appear in the indexed page text, so allowed_domains: [toomeyco.com, ragoarts.com] plus the maker and form still returns dated sold results. That recovered five Handel comps below. It is materially weaker — no control over which lots come back, and it returned nothing for Pairpoint boudoir or Moe Bridges — but it is not nothing.

§3.8 · The one thing learned

Red flag 14 — the same lot, at two houses, at the same impossible estimate

Bidhaus and Mynt are listing each other’s lots

Both rated do not bid11 of 37 lots

Run 1 flagged both houses for hyper-precise estimates at a multiple of market (red flag 13) and rated both do not bid. This run shows what is actually happening underneath that.

Lot titleAppears atEstimate — identicalBid
Pairpoint “Tiffany Style” Poppy Table Lamp Bidhaus · Mynt $32,175–$41,827$100 · $100
Handel Art Nouveau Birds of Paradise Table Lamp Bidhaus · Mynt $14,781–$19,215$100 · $100
Handel Art Deco Elephantine Island Table Lamp Mynt · Mynt again $13,702–$17,812$100 · $100

Three lots, six listings, two houses, estimates matching to the dollar, every one opening at exactly $100. The third pair is the same lot at the same house under two different lot IDs.

What it means. Estimates agreeing to the dollar across two businesses are not two independent valuations of one object — they are one number copied. Combined with the four-significant-figure precision already flagged, the estimates are generated, not judged. And a lot that keeps reappearing at $100 is a lot that never sells: it is being cycled to harvest bids, not to transfer goods.

The operational value is that this is cheap to detect and it condemns a whole seller at once. When the same title and the same estimate appear under two house names, discard everything from both without reading the lots. That removed 11 of 37 LiveAuctioneers results this run for the cost of noticing.
